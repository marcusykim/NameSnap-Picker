How to locate In-App Purchases in NameSnap 2.0 (32):
1. Launch NameSnap. No app account or sign-in is required.
2. In CONTESTANT LIST, paste this 17-name example: Alex, Blair, Casey, Drew, Ellis, Finley, Gray, Harper, Indy, Jordan, Kai, Logan, Morgan, Nico, Oakley, Parker, Quinn
3. Tap ADD THESE NAMES TO POOL. Free use supports up to 16 contestants; adding this 17-name list opens the Upgrade to Unlimited? screen.
4. Tap Unlock Lifetime (localized price) to review NameSnap Unlimited Lifetime, product ID namesnap.unlimited_lifetime_699. Or Monthly opens namesnap.unlimited_monthly_099. Restore Purchases is on the same screen.

The old instructions referring to a gear icon and an upgrade menu were obsolete after the UI redesign; use the steps above. This path was verified in the Release configuration of build 32. Use a sandbox reviewer account without an existing Unlimited entitlement. An already-unlocked Lifetime account will not trigger the free-limit screen. There are no app-side storefront or device restrictions. Paid Apps Agreement is Active.
