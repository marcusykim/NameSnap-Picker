# Fresh release-evidence critique — NameSnap iOS accuracy maintenance

Date: September 29, 2026. Reviewed release evidence for 2.0 (30) and 2.0 (31), including the build-31 Debug winner fixture and subsequent real-phone winner/close endpoints.

**Disposition: the supplied pixels do not demonstrate a current name/number accuracy defect. Build-30 phone captures support a coherent four-contestant draw journey; build-31 fixture and live-phone captures show the winner close control below the readable status area.** A subsequent phone capture shows the modal gone, consistent with the operator-reported immediate X dismissal. Build-31 installation is visually supported. Main-screen status overlap remains visible and is explicitly outside the winner-overlay repair; full visual approval is not issued.

Operator-reported actions are identified separately from direct pixel observations; this critic did not rerun those actions. The existing independent critique was available as context; conclusions below are grounded in the pixels and source QA record rather than its verdict.

## Findings and release boundaries

1. **Duplicate identity chronology is now resolved in the record.** The updated QA report explicitly states that original `#4 Alex` had been consumed but retained, the distinct active `#3 Alex` was deleted, and surviving consumed `#4` was renumbered to `#3` before Reset restored it. That explanation is compatible with the simulator wheel pixels. The stills cannot independently establish the underlying UUID continuity, but the report no longer leaves an apparent contradictory sequence.

2. **The build-31 winner repair passes the bounded rendered check on fixture and phone.** In build 30 the winner close-circle border enters the Wi-Fi/battery band; the phone winner captures reproduce that crowding. Both the build-31 fixture and new live-phone winner image place the full close circle below the readable status area. The winner number, name, supporting text, and both actions remain legible and uncut. The operator reports tapping X at mirrored-image coordinate `[273, 121]` immediately dismissed the live `#2 007` modal; the subsequent image shows the overlay absent. That is consistent supporting endpoint evidence, while the timing and causal hit response come from the operator's tool interaction record rather than these stills. Main-screen status overlap persists visibly in the build-31 post-close image, matching the QA report's explicit limitation.

3. **Device evidence has distinct build-30 and build-31 scopes.** Build 30 supplies the four-row pool, the two live numeric-name winner modals, and the exhausted four-entry history. Build 31 supplies a separate TestFlight installation view, a coherent live `#2 007` winner, and the modal-absent endpoint following the reported X action. The operator also reports build-31 first-row replacement typing succeeded without refocus; no supplied image independently proves that typing/focus sequence. No name/number contradiction is visible in the reviewed endpoints. These checks do not visually prove every intervening mutation, clipboard paste, or exhaustion-button behavior, and the earlier build-30 journey must not be represented as a complete rerun on build 31.

4. **The refreshed automated summary supports the stated aggregate result.** Re-read the updated JSON accompanying the build-31 QA record: it records 41 passed, 0 failed, 0 skipped, on iPhone 16e / iOS 26.3.1 Simulator. It does not enumerate test names, independently establish the 34-model/7-editor split, bind the run to a specific app build, or prove archive/upload status. Those remain separately reported claims. No numerical contradiction exists between this summary and the QA report. This critic did not inspect the full xcresult or execute tests.

5. **Remaining image gaps are not negative results.** Live winner-modal and initial four-row active-pool evidence are now present. Duplicate dialogs, repeat-enabled repeated history, the complete six-row Unicode fixture, deletion transitions, and 16/17-name threshold transitions remain outside the images inspected by this critic. The updated QA report references additional phone mutation images that were not included in this bounded follow-up. Manual paste is accurately disclosed as unverified. The simulator keyboard endpoint and operator's phone-typing report should remain separate evidence classes. Automated coverage and reported live interaction can support the result without pretending all steps are visible here.

## Bounded rubric assessment

The numbered rows, draw-mode controls, result, and history form coherent picker UI. Names and numbers are readable; selected modes and toggle positions are distinguishable. The observed controls use consistent styling and the winner fixture does not truncate the displayed name or its action labels. No device mockup, marketing headline overlay, or store-listing frame is embedded in these captures.

The very large Spin control and celebration illustration reduce the amount of pool/result content visible at once. Bottom-bar occlusion limits some simulator captures; it does not prove the content cannot be scrolled into view. Cross-screen continuity is better supported by the four-row phone pool and final four-entry history, and the duplicate-identity explanation is now recorded. The close target has one operator-reported successful real-device hit with compatible before/after images; broader touch-target behavior, VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, reduced motion, physical-device keyboard avoidance, and complete loading/error/destructive states cannot be evaluated here. Originality against multiple current products, implementation handoff completeness, and a 93/100 whole-product score are outside what this packet can establish.

**No new critical CRUD/winner-identity defect is established by the provided pixels. Build-31 winner safe-area correction is visible on the real phone, and its dismissal is supported by the operator-reported action plus the modal-absent endpoint. Do not convert these limited findings into full release, accessibility, or visual-design approval.** Preserve fixture/build labels and the known main-screen overlap; keep the release record synchronized with the completed device checks without broadening their scope.
