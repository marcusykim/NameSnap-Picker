Paste names, tap Spin, and pick a winner instantly.

NameSnap is built to make random picks feel simple, exciting, and trustworthy.

- Teachers and classrooms
- Creators and livestreams

- Parties and group games

## Features
- Paste names from any list
- Spin a random winner instantly
- No-repeat mode
- Include or skip contestants
- Winner history
- Optional unlimited upgrade for larger sessions
