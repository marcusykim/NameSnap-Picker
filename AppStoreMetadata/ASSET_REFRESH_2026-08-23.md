# NameSnap App Store Asset Refresh — August 23, 2026

## Product story

NameSnap is a single-purpose random picker for teachers, streamers, coaches,
party hosts, and any group that needs a fair draw. The asset sequence follows
the actual experience:

1. Celebrate the winner.
2. Paste a group in one step.
3. Run a fast Quick Pick.
4. Turn the draw into a show with Spin Wheel.
5. Keep no-repeat history clear.
6. Reset picks without retyping the list.

The paywall is deliberately absent from every screenshot, preview, and poster
frame.

## Direction comparison

### A. Utility-first

Large wheel, restrained caption, and conventional feature progression. This is
immediately legible but looks interchangeable with wheel-picker competitors.

### B. Classroom-first

Calmer setup, numbered-pool, privacy, and no-repeat emphasis. This builds trust
but understates the delight that makes NameSnap memorable in a room or stream.

### C. Celebration-first — selected

Lead with the real winner celebration, then prove speed, fairness, and control.
The direction uses NameSnap's actual ink outlines, violet offset shadows,
yellow/tan action surfaces, green/lavender/sky gradients, Rubik Mono One display
type, and original celebration artwork. It does not imitate a competitor's
layout or introduce a visual language that the app does not use.

## Apple requirements used

- One to ten screenshots per device size; PNG/JPEG without alpha.
- Up to three previews per supported device size and language.
- Preview duration: 15–30 seconds.
- H.264 High Profile up to Level 4.0, maximum 30 fps.
- Target video bitrate: 10–12 Mbps.
- Stereo AAC at 44.1 or 48 kHz and 256 kbps target.
- Accepted portrait preview sizes used here: 886×1920 for iPhone and 1200×1600
  for iPad.
