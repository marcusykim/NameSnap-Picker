## NameSnap
**Fair random picks in seconds.**

NameSnap is a fast, playful random name picker for classrooms, giveaways, games, livestreams, and any moment where you need a fair decision without overthinking it.

Paste names, tap Spin, and get a winner instantly.

### What NameSnap helps with
- Classroom participation
- Giveaways and raffles
- Party games and group picks
- Livestream audience contests
- Fast fair decisions with a fun reveal

### Features
- Paste names from any list
- Spin for a random winner in seconds
- No-repeat mode
- Include or skip any contestant
- Winner history
- Optional unlocks for larger or expanded usage

## Privacy Policy
NameSnap is designed to be privacy-first.

### At a glance
- No account required
- No sign-in required
- No analytics tracking
- No ad tracking
- No third-party ads
- Participant names, winner history, and app preferences stay on your device
- Purchases and unlock status are handled through Apple’s App Store / StoreKit

### Accounts
NameSnap does **not** require you to create an account.
You do not need to sign up or log in to use the app.

### What data NameSnap collects
NameSnap does **not** collect personal information for analytics, advertising, or account creation.

### What data may be stored on your device
To provide app functionality, NameSnap may store the following locally on your device:
- Entered participant names
- Winner history
- App preferences (such as no-repeat mode)
- Purchase or unlock state needed for app functionality

This information is used to make the app work properly on your device.

### Purchases and subscriptions
If you purchase a subscription or a lifetime/unlimited unlock, purchase processing and entitlement verification are handled by **Apple** through the **App Store** and **StoreKit**.

NameSnap does not require a NameSnap account for purchases.

### Tracking
NameSnap does not use analytics tracking or ad tracking.
It does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

### Data sharing
NameSnap does not sell your data and does not share your data with advertisers.

### Children’s privacy
NameSnap does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

### Changes to this policy
If this policy changes in a future release, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
